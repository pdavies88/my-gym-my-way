My Gym My Way

Your gear. Your workout.

My Gym My Way is a free iPhone and Android app for people who train at home. Tell it what equipment you own, and it builds workouts you can actually do with it.

  • Free. For real. No paywall, no subscription, no ads.
  • Built from your equipment. Dumbbells, a barbell, bands, a pull-up bar or just bodyweight.
  • Generate or build. Pick a focus and a length, or hand-pick exercises from the library.
  • Video demos. Every exercise has a short demonstration video from a fitness coach on YouTube.
  • Private by design. No account, no servers, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone.
