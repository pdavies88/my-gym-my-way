My Gym My Way

Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

The short version: My Gym My Way collects nothing. It has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app works offline and sends none of your data anywhere. Your equipment, workouts, history and body measurements stay on your phone, and I, the developer, never receive any of it.

Who this policy covers

My Gym My Way is an iPhone and Android app made by Preston Davies, an independent developer. This policy explains what the app does with your information. If you have questions, email preston5@protonmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app stores these things only on your device, inside the app's private storage:

  • Your equipment. The list of gear you own, used to pick exercises you can do.
  • Your saved workouts. The workouts you generate or build and choose to keep.
  • Your history. For each finished session, the date, how long it took and which exercises you completed. The app does not track reps or weights.
  • Your body measurements, if you enter them. Your height and weight are used on your phone to calculate your BMI. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Your settings. Whether you use US or metric units.

You can delete any saved workout or past session in the app. If you delete the app, your phone deletes all of its data.

If you back up your phone, the backup can include the app's data. That means iCloud or a computer on iPhone, and Google's Android backup on Android. Those backups are covered by Apple's or Google's own privacy policies.

What leaves your phone

Nothing. The exercise library and the 3D demonstrations are built into the app, so it works fully offline. The only time the app uses the internet is when you tap the privacy policy or support link in Me → About. That opens this website in an in-app browser, and GitHub, which hosts the site, receives a normal web request. GitHub's privacy statement covers its servers.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect any data for the developer or for anyone else.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools.
  • It does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not sell or share your data.
  • It does not require an account or ask for your name, email or location.
  • It does not ask for access to your camera, photos, contacts, location or health data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Health information

The workouts, exercise demonstrations and BMI calculator are general fitness information for adults. They are not medical advice, and the app is not a medical device. BMI is a rough screening number. It does not account for muscle mass, age, sex or body shape. Talk to a doctor before you start a new exercise program, especially if you have a health condition, an injury or are pregnant. Stop exercising if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and its effective date. Major changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
